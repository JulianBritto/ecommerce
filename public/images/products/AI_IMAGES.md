# Imágenes IA para TechStore

Coloca las imágenes en `public/images/products/` con estos nombres exactos (recomendado: **WEBP**, 1200×800 o 1600×1067).

Si un archivo no existe, el sitio mostrará el SVG placeholder automáticamente.

## Archivos esperados

- `pc-gamer-pulse.webp`
- `pc-estudio.webp`
- `laptop-creator-16.webp`
- `laptop-pro-14.webp`
- `laptop-gamer-15.webp`
- `monitor-27-165hz.webp`

## Prompts (estilo consistente “foto de producto”)

Usa estos prompts en cualquier generador (DALL·E, Midjourney, Stable Diffusion, etc.).

### 1) PC Gamer
**Prompt**
"Photorealistic product photo of a modern gaming desktop PC tower with subtle RGB lighting, tempered glass side panel, clean minimal white studio background, soft shadow under the product, 3/4 angle view, ultra-detailed, sharp focus, e-commerce packshot"

**Negative (si aplica)**
"text, watermark, logo, brand names, people, hands, messy background, low resolution, blurry"

### 2) PC Estudio
"Photorealistic product photo of a minimalist desktop PC tower, no RGB, quiet workstation look, clean white studio background, soft shadow, 3/4 angle, ultra-detailed, e-commerce packshot"

### 3) Laptop (Creator / Pro)
"Photorealistic product photo of a sleek modern laptop open at 110 degrees, thin bezels, clean white studio background, soft shadow, 3/4 angle, ultra-detailed, e-commerce packshot, no logos, no text"

### 4) Laptop Gamer
"Photorealistic product photo of a gaming laptop open, subtle RGB keyboard glow, aggressive but clean design, white studio background, soft shadow, 3/4 angle, ultra-detailed, e-commerce packshot, no logos"

### 5) Monitor
"Photorealistic product photo of a 27-inch gaming monitor on a stand, screen with abstract colorful gradient (no text), white studio background, soft shadow, 3/4 angle, ultra-detailed, e-commerce packshot"

### 6) Teclado mecánico
"Photorealistic product photo of a compact mechanical keyboard, subtle RGB, clean white studio background, soft shadow, top-front angle, ultra-detailed, e-commerce packshot"

### 7) Mouse
"Photorealistic product photo of a modern gaming mouse, matte finish, clean white studio background, soft shadow, 3/4 angle, ultra-detailed, e-commerce packshot"

### 8) Tarjeta gráfica (GPU)
"Photorealistic product photo of a modern graphics card with 3 fans, clean white studio background, soft shadow, 3/4 angle, ultra-detailed, e-commerce packshot, no logos"

### 9) SSD NVMe
"Photorealistic product photo of an NVMe M.2 SSD on white studio background, soft shadow, macro product shot, ultra-detailed, e-commerce packshot, no readable text"

## Recomendación

Para que todas las imágenes se vean coherentes:
- Fondo blanco/gris muy claro
- Misma iluminación (softbox)
- Misma perspectiva (3/4)
- Sin logos ni textos
